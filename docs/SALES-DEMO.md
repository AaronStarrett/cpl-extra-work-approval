# Sales demonstration

Paul's pitch: **“When a customer asks for something extra, send a clear scope and price, get their response, and keep the record together.”**

The short line is **“Agree on the extra work before it starts.”** Attribute the product **By Cyber Pirate Labs**.

## A truthful three-minute walkthrough

Start at the blank app. Explain that saving creates a private workspace and that the management link must stay with the contractor. Use a fresh, clearly fictional request only in an explicitly chosen demonstration/test workspace; never seed a real visitor's workspace.

The fictional example is a side gate added to a fencing job for **$350 additional** with **one additional working day**. The figure is an illustrative amount, not market-rate or tax advice. Enter the scope, included work, exclusions and timing; show that no original-contract grand total is being changed. Preview is read-only.

Publish, then copy the customer link. Say “The link is copied; I can paste it into my existing conversation.” Do not say an email or text was sent. Keep management and customer capabilities out of the recording.

Open the review in an independent browser context. Show the exact version, the three choices and the unchecked acknowledgment. Type a fictional name, confirm approval, and wait for the server receipt. Refresh the owner ledger and export the stored result. Describe this as a synthetic verification of real persistence only if that run actually happened.

For the declined branch, show a separate final response that does not authorize work. For the requested-changes branch, enter “Please use a latch that can be opened from both sides.” Explain that it is not approval; a revised version needs a fresh response. Do not turn branch selection in an illustrative portfolio player into a live database mutation.

Close by showing the record's retention deadline and browser **Print / Save as PDF**. The tool records a response from a bearer-link holder; it does not verify identity, collect payment, certify signatures or guarantee legal effect.
